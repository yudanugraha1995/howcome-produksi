# What If You Sat Close to the TV Every Day?

Total: 1177 characters incl. tags (max 1200). YouTube = full narration + silent scenes (144 s). Short = cut to 55 s.

## hook (SHORT + YT)

[scoffs] Move back! You're sitting way too close to the TV! [curious] Sound familiar? What if you really sat super close to the screen every single day? Would it ruin your eyes?

## context (YT only)

Almost every kid has heard this warning from their parents. Some were told they would need glasses, or even go blind.

## point1 (YT only)

Here's what really happens. Staring at a screen up close makes you blink less, so your eyes can feel dry, tired, and itchy. Kids also focus up close more easily than adults do.

## point2 (SHORT + YT)

[surprised] But eye doctors say sitting close to the TV won't permanently damage your eyes. It can cause eye strain, but that goes away with rest. What really matters for kids is time outside. In one big school study, adding about forty minutes of outdoor time a day lowered the number of kids who became nearsighted.

## twist (SHORT + YT)

[laughs] Plot twist! The warning came from a real problem. In the nineteen sixties, some TVs gave off extra radiation, and they were recalled. Today's screens are safe. [excited] For tired eyes, try twenty twenty twenty: every twenty minutes, look twenty feet away for twenty seconds.
