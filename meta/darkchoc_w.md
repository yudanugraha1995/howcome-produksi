# What If You Ate Dark Chocolate Every Day? 🍫

## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Ate Dark Chocolate Every Day? 🍫

Description:
What if you ate a piece of dark chocolate every day? 🍫

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
It's often called the healthy kind of chocolate. Dark chocolate is packed with flavanols, plant compounds linked to better blood flow and heart health. The darker it is, usually seventy percent cocoa or more, the more flavanols and the less sugar.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Tests found lead and cadmium in many popular dark chocolate bars, so eating a lot every day isn't a great idea. Experts say a small square or two a day is plenty, and switching brands helps. It's also still high in calories and fat, so more isn't always better.
