Title: What If Your Toothbrush Sat Next to the Toilet? 🪥 #shorts

Description:
Your toothbrush sits right next to the toilet. 😬 What if it stayed there every day?
Every flush sprays tiny droplets into the air. In one study of shared bathrooms, most toothbrushes had poop germs on them!
Plot twist: a closed case can make it worse because it stays wet. Let it air dry, and close the lid when you flush.

Where's your toothbrush? 👇

Source: Aber et al., American Society for Microbiology (2015) · American Dental Association
