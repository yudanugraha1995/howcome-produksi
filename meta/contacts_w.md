## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Slept in Your Contact Lenses? 👁️

Description:
What if you slept in your contact lenses every night? 👁️

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Millions of people wear contact lenses every day instead of glasses. Your eyes would feel dry in the morning. Your cornea gets oxygen straight from the air, and a lens on top blocks some of it.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Sleeping in contacts makes eye infections about six to eight times more likely, because your eyes get less oxygen, and some infections can damage your vision! Even lenses approved for overnight wear raise the risk, so eye doctors say take them out before bed. One rare infection comes from a tiny amoeba found in water, so never rinse your lenses with tap water.
