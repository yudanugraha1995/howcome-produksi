🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you spent one normal day living like an American? Visitors notice these five habits right away. One, they put lots of ice in almost every drink, even in winter. Two, they say how are you as a quick hello, and nobody expects a real answer. Three, many homes have a giant fridge, and lots of families keep a second one in the garage. But here's the twist. Four, the price on the tag isn't what you pay, because sales tax gets added at the register. Five, a fifteen to twenty percent tip is expected, since a server's base pay can be just two dollars thirteen an hour. So a ten dollar meal can easily end up costing over thirteen dollars.
