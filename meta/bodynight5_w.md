Description:
What if you could watch your body while you sleep? 😴

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Every night, it does some very strange things. One, your brain cleans itself, flushing out waste while you're deep asleep. Two, your muscles go nearly paralyzed during dreams, so you don't act them out. Three, you lose water just from breathing and sweating, so you wake up a little lighter.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Four, your body temperature drops by about one degree Celsius. Five, that sudden falling feeling is called a hypnic jerk, and most people get it sometimes. And you're actually a little taller in the morning, because your spine gets a break from gravity.
