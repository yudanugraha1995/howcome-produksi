## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Bit Your Nails Every Day? 💅

Description:
What if you bit your nails every day? 💅

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Nail biting is super common, and it often starts in childhood when people feel bored or stressed. Your fingers carry tons of germs, and biting moves them straight into your mouth. Germs love hiding under your nails, where washing often misses them.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Years of nail biting can chip and wear down your front teeth, and even damage the nail bed, so nails grow back bumpy. Some people paint on a bitter tasting nail polish to break the habit! Doctors call severe nail biting onychophagia, and a stress ball or fidget toy can help keep your hands busy.
