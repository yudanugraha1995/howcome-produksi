What if you only spoke American English for a day? These phrases confuse visitors all the time. One, how are you doing, which is just hello, not a real question. Two, I'm good, which Americans say even when they're not. Three, we should hang out sometime, which often isn't a real plan. But here's the twist. Four, Americans ask for the restroom or the bathroom, even when there's no bath and no rest. Five, a quarter till four, which means three forty five and confuses lots of learners.
