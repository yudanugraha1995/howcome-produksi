Title: What If You Chewed Your Pen Every Day? 🖊️

Description:
What if you chewed on your pen every single day? 🖊️

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Students and office workers do it the most. Lots of people do it while thinking, and it can feel calming. Chewing is a common way people release nervous energy.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Pens can carry tons of germs, and biting hard plastic can crack teeth and wear down fillings. Swallowing a broken pen cap is also a real choking risk, especially for kids. That's why many pen caps have a tiny hole in the top, to let air through.
