Title: What If You Slept in Socks Every Night? 🧦 #shorts

Description:
Cold feet under the blanket? 🧦 What if you slept in socks every night?
Warm feet open blood vessels so heat leaves your body faster, and a cooler body falls asleep sooner. In one study, sock wearers fell asleep faster.
Plot twist: warm feet were one of the best signs someone was about to fall asleep!

Socks or bare feet? 👇

Source: Ko & Lee, J Physiol Anthropol (2018) · Kräuchi et al., Nature (1999)
