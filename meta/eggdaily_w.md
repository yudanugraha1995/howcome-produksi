What if you ate an egg every single day? 🥚

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
It's one of the cheapest breakfasts on the planet. One egg has about six grams of protein, plus vitamin D, B12 and choline for your brain. The yolk holds most of those vitamins, so skipping it means skipping the good stuff.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Eggs were feared for their cholesterol for decades, but research now shows that for most healthy people, one egg a day doesn't raise heart disease risk. For most people, the saturated fat in foods like bacon and butter affects blood cholesterol more than the egg itself. If you have diabetes or high cholesterol, ask your doctor first.

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you ate an egg every single day? It's one of the cheapest breakfasts on the planet. One egg has about six grams of protein, plus vitamin D, B12 and choline for your brain. The yolk holds most of those vitamins, so skipping it means skipping the good stuff. But here's the twist. Eggs were feared for their cholesterol for decades, but research now shows that for most healthy people, one egg a day doesn't raise heart disease risk. For most people, the saturated fat in foods like bacon and butter affects blood cholesterol more than the egg itself. If you have diabetes or high cholesterol, ask your doctor first.
