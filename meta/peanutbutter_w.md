Title: What If You Ate Peanut Butter Every Day? 🥜

Description:
What if you ate peanut butter every single day? 🥜

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
It's one of the most loved spreads in America. Two tablespoons give you about seven grams of protein and healthy fats that keep you full. Peanuts also have fiber, magnesium and vitamin E.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Those same two tablespoons have nearly two hundred calories, and many brands add sugar, salt and oils. Experts say pick a jar with just peanuts and a little salt, and stir the oil back in. And it's easy to eat way more than two tablespoons straight from the jar!
