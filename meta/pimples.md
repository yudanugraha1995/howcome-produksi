Title: What If You Popped Every Pimple? 😬 #shorts

Description:
A big pimple right on your nose, the day before a party. 😬 What if you popped every single one? KOMO zooms into your skin to see what really happens.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
A pimple forms when a pore gets clogged with oil (sebum) and dead skin cells, and bacteria grow inside. Your immune system attacks, causing redness and swelling. Squeezing it can push the oil, bacteria and pus deeper into the skin instead of out. That can make the pimple bigger and more inflamed, and it can leave scars or dark spots that last for months.

📌 KEY FACTS
• Dermatologists recommend not popping pimples.
• Squeezing can push bacteria deeper and cause more swelling.
• Popping raises the chance of acne scars and dark spots (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation).
• Hydrocolloid pimple patches can protect a spot and stop picking.
• Acne is very common, especially in teens, and is not caused by being dirty.
• Persistent or painful acne can be treated, so see a doctor or dermatologist.

✅ HANDS OFF PLAN
• Use a warm compress to calm the spot.
• Try a pimple patch so you do not pick.
• Wash gently twice a day; scrubbing hard can make it worse.
• Look for products with benzoyl peroxide or salicylic acid.
• Painful, deep or scarring acne? See a dermatologist.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Is it ever okay to pop a pimple?
A: Dermatologists can safely drain some spots; at home it is best to leave them alone.
Q: Does chocolate cause acne?
A: The evidence is mixed; for some people, high-sugar diets may play a role.
Q: Does toothpaste dry pimples?
A: It can irritate skin; use proper acne products instead.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Blackheads are not dirt! The black color is skin oil that turned dark when it met the air, just like a cut apple turns brown. 🍎
