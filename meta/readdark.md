Title: What If You Read in the Dark Every Night? 📖 #shorts

Description:
What if you read in the dark every night? 📖🌙 Mom always said you would ruin your eyes... but is that actually true? KOMO checks what eye doctors really say.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
When the light is dim, your pupils open wide and the tiny focusing muscles inside your eyes work harder to keep the words sharp. You also stare more and blink less. That combination makes your eyes feel tired, dry, achy or a little blurry. It is called eye strain, and it is uncomfortable but temporary. Your eyes are not being permanently damaged; once you rest them, they recover.

📌 KEY FACTS
• Eye doctors (American Academy of Ophthalmology) say reading in dim light does not cause lasting damage to your eyes.
• Dim light can cause eye strain: tired, dry, sore or blurry eyes, and sometimes a headache.
• When you focus on a book or a screen, you blink much less than normal, so the surface of your eyes dries out.
• Eye strain goes away with rest. It does not make your eyesight worse over time.
• Nearsightedness (myopia) is becoming more common in kids around the world, and lifestyle plays a big role.
• Studies in schoolchildren found that more time outdoors in daylight is linked to fewer kids becoming nearsighted.

✅ EASY HABITS FOR HAPPY EYES
• Use a lamp that lights up the page evenly; avoid a tiny bright spot in a dark room.
• Try the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds.
• Blink on purpose when your eyes feel dry, and take short breaks during long reading sessions.
• Get outside in daylight every day, especially kids.
• If blurry vision does not go away after rest, get an eye exam.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Can reading in the dark make me need glasses?
A: No good evidence says so. It causes temporary eye strain, not lasting damage.
Q: Is reading on a phone in the dark different?
A: Screens add glare and you may blink even less, so dryness and strain can feel worse; turn the brightness to match the room and take breaks.
Q: Why do my eyes feel blurry after reading?
A: The focusing muscles get tired and the eye surface dries out. Rest and blinking usually fix it.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
The real risk for kids is not the dark bedroom, it is spending too much time indoors. Kids who play outside every day are less likely to need glasses! Daylight seems to help young eyes grow the right shape. ☀️
