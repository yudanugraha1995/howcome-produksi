Title: What If You Read in the Dark Every Night? 📖 #shorts

Description:
What if you read in the dark every night? Mom always said you'd ruin your eyes! 📖🌙
Good news: eye doctors say dim light won't damage your eyes for good. It just makes them work harder, so they feel tired and blurry. You also blink less when you focus, so your eyes dry out. Rest them every 20 minutes and they bounce right back.
Plot twist: the real risk is staying indoors. Kids who play outside every day are less likely to need glasses! ☀️

Lamp on, or reading in the dark? 👇

Source: American Academy of Ophthalmology
