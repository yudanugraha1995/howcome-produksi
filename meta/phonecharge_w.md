# What If You Charged Your Phone Overnight Every Night? 🔋

## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Charged Your Phone Overnight Every Night? 🔋

Description:
What if you charged your phone overnight every single night? 🔋

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Most people plug in right before bed. Modern phones stop charging once they're full, so it won't explode. That's why many phones now have an optimized charging setting that waits to finish until you wake up.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Sitting at one hundred percent for hours, especially when it's warm, slowly wears out the battery faster. Battery experts say keeping it between about twenty and eighty percent helps it last longer. And never charge it under your pillow, because trapped heat is the real danger.
