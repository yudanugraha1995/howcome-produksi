Description:
What if you held your pee every day? 🚽

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Teachers, nurses, drivers and gamers often hold it for hours. Your bladder stretches like a balloon and can hold about two cups. When it's about half full, your bladder sends the first signal to go.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Holding it too often can weaken your bladder muscles over time and raise your risk of urinary tract infections. Most adults pee about six to eight times a day, so going when you feel the urge is the healthy choice. In rare cases, holding it way too long can stretch the bladder so much that it struggles to empty.
