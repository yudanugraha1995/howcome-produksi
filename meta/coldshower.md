Title: What If You Took a Cold Shower Every Day? 🚿 #shorts

Description:
Freezing water, first thing in the morning. 🥶 Would it change anything?
In the Netherlands, ~3,000 people ended their hot shower with cold water for a month.
The cold groups took ~29% fewer sick days off work, but they didn't get sick less often. 😳
Plot twist: 30 seconds worked about as well as 90!

Sources: Buijze et al., PLOS ONE (2016)
