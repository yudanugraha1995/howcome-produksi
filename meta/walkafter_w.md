Title: What If You Walked After Every Meal? 🚶

Description:
What if you went for a short walk after every meal? 🚶

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
In many cultures, a walk after dinner is an old tradition. Just ten minutes of walking helps your muscles use the sugar from your food. It can also help with digestion and that heavy, sleepy feeling.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Studies found that even two to five minutes of light walking after eating can lower blood sugar spikes better than sitting. That's why some doctors call it one of the easiest health habits there is. Just keep it gentle, not a hard run right after a big plate.

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you went for a short walk after every meal? In many cultures, a walk after dinner is an old tradition. Just ten minutes of walking helps your muscles use the sugar from your food. It can also help with digestion and that heavy, sleepy feeling. But here's the twist. Studies found that even two to five minutes of light walking after eating can lower blood sugar spikes better than sitting. That's why some doctors call it one of the easiest health habits there is.
