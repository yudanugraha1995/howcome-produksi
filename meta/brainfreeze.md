Title: What If You Got Brain Freeze Every Time? 🍦 #shorts

Description:
Ow, ow, ow! You ate ice cream too fast. 🍦🥶 What if you got brain freeze every single time? KOMO explains what is really happening inside your head, and the trick that stops it.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
Brain freeze (doctors call it a "sphenopalatine ganglioneuralgia" or cold-stimulus headache) starts in your mouth, not your brain. Something very cold touches the roof of your mouth and the back of your throat. The blood vessels there react fast: they tighten, then quickly widen again to bring warm blood back. Nerves in that area send the signal to your brain, and your brain reads it as pain coming from your forehead. That is why a cold snack feels like a stab between your eyes.

📌 KEY FACTS
• Brain freeze usually lasts from a few seconds to under a minute.
• In a lab study, volunteers sipped ice water through a straw against the roof of the mouth to trigger brain freeze on purpose.
• Researchers saw a sudden increase of blood flow in an artery in the brain right when the pain started, and the pain faded when it narrowed again.
• The pain is "referred": the problem is in your mouth, but your brain feels it in your forehead.
• Eating or drinking cold things slowly makes brain freeze much less likely.
• People who get migraines may get brain freeze more easily.

✅ HOW TO STOP IT FAST
• Press your tongue against the roof of your mouth to warm it up.
• Sip a little warm or room-temperature water.
• Slow down: smaller bites, slower sips.
• Let the cold food sit in the front of your mouth for a moment first.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Is brain freeze dangerous?
A: No. It is a short, harmless headache that goes away by itself.
Q: Why does it hurt in my forehead?
A: The nerves from the roof of your mouth share pathways with nerves from your face, so the brain feels the pain in your forehead.
Q: Can hot food cause it too?
A: No, brain freeze is triggered by cold.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
The quickest fix is already in your mouth: your tongue! Pushing it up against the roof of your mouth warms the cold spot, and the pain often fades in seconds. 👅
