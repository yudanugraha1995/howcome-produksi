Title: What If You Wore Headphones All Day? 🎧 #shorts

Description:
Headphones on the bus, at work, at the gym, in bed... 🎧 What happens to your ears?
Tiny hair cells in your ear turn sound into signals, and loud noise can break them. They don't grow back. 😳
Simple rule: under 60% volume, about 60 minutes at a time.
Plot twist: noise cancelling headphones can protect your ears, because you don't need to crank it up!
