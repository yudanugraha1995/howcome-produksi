Title: What If You Wore Headphones All Day? 🎧 #shorts

Description:
Headphones on the bus, at work, at the gym, even in bed... 🎧 What if you wore them all day, every day? KOMO zooms into your inner ear to see what loud music really does.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
Deep inside your ear is the cochlea, lined with thousands of tiny hair cells. They move when sound waves hit them and turn that movement into signals for your brain. Very loud sound, or loud sound for a long time, can bend and damage these hair cells. In humans, they do not grow back. Damage builds up slowly and often without pain, so many people do not notice until they have ringing ears (tinnitus) or trouble hearing in noisy places.

📌 KEY FACTS
• The World Health Organization estimated over 1 billion young people are at risk of hearing loss from unsafe listening.
• Damaged inner-ear hair cells do not regrow in humans.
• How much risk depends on both loudness and time: louder sound means a much shorter safe time.
• Ringing in your ears after loud music is a warning sign.
• Many phones now have hearing-health settings that track your weekly sound exposure.
• Listening in noisy places makes people turn the volume up higher.

✅ SAFE LISTENING
• Use the 60/60 rule: under 60% volume, about 60 minutes at a time, then take a break.
• Turn on your phone volume limit or hearing-health tracking.
• Choose noise-cancelling headphones so you do not need to crank it up.
• If your ears ring or feel muffled, give them quiet time.
• Get your hearing checked if conversations start sounding unclear.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Are earbuds worse than over-ear headphones?
A: Volume and time matter most, but earbuds sit closer to the eardrum and are easier to play too loud.
Q: Can hearing come back after damage?
A: Temporary muffling can recover, but permanent hair-cell damage does not.
Q: Is sleeping with earbuds bad?
A: Long hours of sound and pressure in the ear can be a problem; keep it quiet and short.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Noise-cancelling headphones can actually protect your ears! They block outside noise, so you do not need to turn the music up as loud. 🎧
