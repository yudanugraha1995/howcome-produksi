## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Used Hand Sanitizer All Day? 🧴

Description:
What if you used hand sanitizer all day long? 🧴

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Lots of people keep a little bottle in every bag. Alcohol based sanitizer kills most germs on your hands in about thirty seconds. Experts say look for one with at least sixty percent alcohol.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
It doesn't work well on greasy or dirty hands, and it struggles against some germs like norovirus, so soap and water is still better. Using it constantly can also dry out and crack your skin. Washing with soap for twenty seconds physically rinses germs away.
