Description:
What if you cracked your neck every day? 💥

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Lots of people do it when their neck feels tight after sitting at a desk. That pop is just gas bubbles in your joints. When the joint stretches, pressure drops and the gas forms a bubble that pops.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Twisting your neck hard over and over can stretch the ligaments, and in rare cases even injure the arteries in your neck. So go gentle! If your neck feels stiff every day, gentle stretches are a safer fix. Doctors say if cracking comes with pain or dizziness, it's time to get it checked.
