# What If You Breathed Through Your Mouth All Night? 😮

## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Breathed Through Your Mouth All Night? 😮

Description:
What if you breathed through your mouth all night? 😮

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Your body is built to breathe through your nose, day and night. You'd wake up with a dry mouth. A stuffy nose or allergies are often the reason.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Your nose filters, warms and moistens the air, so mouth breathing can lead to bad breath, cavities and snoring! It can also make sleep apnea worse, so a blocked nose is worth checking. In kids, long term mouth breathing can even affect how the face and teeth grow.
