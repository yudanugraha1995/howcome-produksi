🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you double dipped your chip every single time? It's one of the most argued about snack habits ever. It feels harmless, just one more dip. Some people even flip the chip around to dip the other side. But here's the twist. A Clemson University study found that double dipping can move thousands of bacteria from your mouth into the dip, especially into thin dips like salsa. Thick dips like cheese dip picked up fewer bacteria, because less of it drips back into the bowl. Most mouth germs are harmless, but if someone is sick, it can spread illness.
