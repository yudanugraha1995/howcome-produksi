Title: What If You Sat Close to the TV Every Day? 📺 #shorts

Description:
"Move back! You're too close to the TV!" 📺 Would it really ruin your eyes?
Eye doctors say sitting close won't permanently damage your eyes. It can cause eye strain, which goes away with rest.
What matters more for kids: time outside. ~40 extra minutes outdoors a day meant fewer kids became nearsighted. 🌳
Plot twist: the warning came from real 1960s TVs that gave off extra radiation, and were recalled!

Were you told to move back from the TV? 👇

Sources: American Academy of Ophthalmology · He et al., JAMA (2015)
