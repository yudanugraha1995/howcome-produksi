What if you ate a banana every single day? 🍌

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
It's one of the most eaten fruits on the planet. One banana gives you potassium, fiber and vitamin B6, and it's an easy energy boost. That potassium helps your muscles and heart work properly.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Bananas are slightly radioactive because of their potassium, but you'd need to eat millions at once for it to be dangerous. Scientists even joke about the banana equivalent dose to explain radiation. Your body also keeps potassium levels balanced, so extra just gets flushed out.
