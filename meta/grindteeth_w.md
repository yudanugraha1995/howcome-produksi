# What If You Ground Your Teeth Every Night? 😬

## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Ground Your Teeth Every Night? 😬

Description:
What if you ground your teeth every night? 😬

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Doctors call it bruxism, and partners often notice the grinding sound first. Most people who do it don't even know. Stress and sleep problems can make it worse.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Your jaw can clench with a lot of force while you sleep, which can wear teeth down, crack them, and cause morning headaches and jaw pain. A dentist can make a night guard to protect your teeth while you sleep. Some people also clench during the day without noticing, especially when they're focused or stressed.
