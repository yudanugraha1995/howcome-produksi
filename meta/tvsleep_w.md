What if you fell asleep with the TV on every night? Many people grew up falling asleep to the glow of a TV. The noise feels relaxing. Lots of people use it to fall asleep faster. But here's the twist. The light and changing sounds can keep pulling your brain out of deep sleep, and a big study linked sleeping with a TV or light on to weight gain over time!
