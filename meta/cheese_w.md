Title: What If You Ate Cheese Every Day? 🧀

Description:
What if you ate cheese every single day? 🧀

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
It's on pizza, burgers and sandwiches everywhere. Cheese is loaded with calcium and protein, which help build strong bones and muscles. Hard cheeses like parmesan are also very low in lactose.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Big studies found that people who eat a little cheese daily didn't have a higher heart risk, and some aged cheeses even contain good bacteria for your gut. But cheese is also high in salt and saturated fat, so portion size matters. One portion is about the size of three dice.
