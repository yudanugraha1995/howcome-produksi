Earbuds in, playlist on, lights off. 🎶 Good for sleep?
Music can relax you, but in one study, people who listened to more music near bedtime got songs stuck in their heads at night and slept worse.
Plot twist: even calm songs without words caused night earworms!

Source: Scullin et al., Psychological Science (2021)
