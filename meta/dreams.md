Title: What If You Remembered Every Dream? 💭 #shorts

Description:
You had the wildest dream... and two minutes later it is gone. 💭 What if you remembered every dream? KOMO explains why your brain forgets them so fast.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
You go through several sleep cycles every night, and you dream in many of them, especially during REM sleep (rapid eye movement). During REM, parts of your brain are very active, but the systems that save memories work differently than when you are awake. So unless you wake up during or right after a dream and think about it, the memory fades within minutes.

📌 KEY FACTS
• Most people have several dream periods every night, even if they remember none.
• The most vivid, story-like dreams usually happen in REM sleep.
• Waking up during a dream makes it much easier to remember.
• Some people naturally recall dreams more often; studies link it to more brief awakenings at night.
• Writing dreams down as soon as you wake up helps you remember more of them.
• Lucid dreaming means knowing you are dreaming while it happens.

✅ WANT TO REMEMBER MORE DREAMS?
• Keep a notebook or notes app next to your bed.
• Before moving, lie still and replay the dream in your head.
• Write down even tiny pieces: a color, a place, a feeling.
• Get enough sleep: REM periods get longer toward the morning.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Does everyone dream?
A: Almost everyone does, even people who say they never dream; they just do not remember.
Q: Why do dreams feel so real?
A: Areas for emotion and vision are very active in REM, while areas for logic are quieter.
Q: Do dreams mean something?
A: Scientists are still debating; they may help process emotions and memories.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
People who were born blind still dream! Their dreams are full of sounds, smells, touch and emotions instead of pictures. 🎧👃
