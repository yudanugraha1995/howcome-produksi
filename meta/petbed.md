Title: What If Your Pet Slept in Your Bed Every Night? 🐶 #shorts

Description:
Your dog jumps on the bed and steals your spot. 🐶🛏️ What if your pet slept in your bed every night? Good or bad for your sleep? KOMO checks a real sleep study.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
Researchers at the Mayo Clinic tracked the sleep of people and their dogs with activity trackers for a week. They compared dogs sleeping in the bedroom but off the bed with dogs sleeping on the human bed. Pets move, stretch, scratch and wake up during the night, and those little movements can nudge you out of deeper sleep without you noticing.

📌 KEY FACTS
• In the Mayo Clinic study, people slept well with a dog in the bedroom.
• When the dog was on the bed, people slept a little less efficiently.
• Dogs and cats have different sleep cycles than humans and move more at night.
• Many owners say they feel calmer and safer with their pet nearby.
• People with allergies or asthma may feel worse with pets in bed.
• Cats are often active at dawn, which can mean early wake-up calls.

✅ SHARE THE ROOM, NOT THE PILLOW
• Try a comfy pet bed next to your bed.
• Wash bedding often if your pet sleeps with you.
• Keep pets clean and protected from fleas and ticks.
• If you have allergies, keep pets out of the bed.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Is it unhygienic to sleep with a dog?
A: Healthy, clean pets are usually fine; wash bedding often and keep up flea treatment.
Q: Does sleeping with a pet help anxiety?
A: Many people say it feels comforting.
Q: Should puppies sleep in your bed?
A: Many trainers suggest a crate or pet bed first.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Even if pets move around, many people still say they sleep happier with their pet close by. Comfort counts too! 🐾
