## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Didn't Shower for a Week? 🚿

Description:
What if you didn't shower for a week? 🚿

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Your body makes sweat and skin oil every single day. At first, you'd just feel greasy. Your scalp would get oily and itchy too.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Sweat itself doesn't smell. The smell comes from bacteria on your skin eating that sweat, and after a week, dead skin can even build up in dark patches! Dermatologists say most people are fine showering daily or every other day, as long as you wash the sweaty spots. Rinsing off sweat after the gym matters most, because sweaty skin is where acne and rashes start.

What if you didn't shower for a week? Your body makes sweat and skin oil every single day. At first, you'd just feel greasy. Your scalp would get oily and itchy too. But here's the twist. Sweat itself doesn't smell. The smell comes from bacteria on your skin eating that sweat, and after a week, dead skin can even build up in dark patches! Dermatologists say most people are fine showering daily or every other day, as long as you wash the sweaty spots.
