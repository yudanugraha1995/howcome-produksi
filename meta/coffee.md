Title: What If You Drank Coffee Every Day? ☕ #shorts

Description:
One cup in the morning, one after lunch, one more at 4 PM... ☕ What if you drank coffee every single day? Is it hurting you or helping you? KOMO checks the science.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
Caffeine works by blocking adenosine, a chemical that builds up in your brain during the day and makes you feel sleepy. With adenosine blocked, you feel more alert. Caffeine stays in your body for hours: about half of it is still there 5 or more hours later, which is why a late coffee can mess with your sleep. Coffee also contains many other plant compounds, which is one reason scientists study its long-term effects.

📌 KEY FACTS
• A big BMJ review of 200+ studies found people who drank about 3-4 cups a day had a lower risk of dying early and of heart disease than non-drinkers.
• That is a link, not proof: coffee drinkers may differ in other ways too.
• The US FDA says up to about 400 mg of caffeine a day is generally fine for most healthy adults (roughly 4 cups of brewed coffee).
• Pregnant people are usually advised to keep caffeine lower; check with a doctor.
• Coffee late in the day can make it harder to fall asleep and reduce deep sleep.
• Sugar and syrups can turn a coffee into a dessert, so the toppings matter.

✅ SMART COFFEE HABITS
• Keep it moderate and stop several hours before bedtime.
• Go easy on sugar, syrups and whipped cream.
• If coffee makes you jittery or your heart races, cut back.
• Kids and teens should avoid caffeine-heavy drinks.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Does coffee stunt your growth?
A: No good evidence shows that, but kids and teens should still keep caffeine low.
Q: How late is too late for coffee?
A: Many sleep experts suggest stopping at least 6 hours before bed.
Q: Is decaf caffeine-free?
A: Almost, but it still has a small amount.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Coffee does not really dehydrate you! In one study, moderate daily coffee hydrated people about as well as water. 💧
