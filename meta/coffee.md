Title: What If You Drank Coffee Every Day? ☕ #shorts

Description:
One cup in the morning, one after lunch, one more at 4... ☕ Hurting you or helping you?
A review of 200+ studies found people who drank ~3-4 cups a day had a lower risk of dying early and of heart disease than non-drinkers. A link, not proof, and go easy on the sugar!
Plot twist: coffee doesn't really dehydrate you. It hydrated about as well as water in one study! 💧
