Title: What If You Never Cut Your Hair? 💇 #shorts

Description:
No haircut. Ever again. 💇 What if you never cut your hair? How long would it really get? KOMO does the math.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
Scalp hair grows about 1 cm a month, roughly 15 cm a year. But every hair follows a life cycle: a growing phase (anagen) that usually lasts a few years, then a short resting phase, and then it falls out and a new one starts. Because the growing phase has a limit, each hair can only reach a certain length. For many people that is somewhere around the shoulders to the waist, but people with very long growing phases can grow much more.

📌 KEY FACTS
• Scalp hair grows about 1 cm (0.4 inches) per month on average.
• The growing phase (anagen) usually lasts about 2 to 7 years.
• Losing around 50 to 100 hairs a day is normal.
• Your maximum hair length depends mostly on how long your growing phase lasts.
• Hair grows a bit faster in warm months for some people.
• Trims do not make hair grow faster, but they remove split ends so it looks healthier.

✅ HEALTHY LONG HAIR TIPS
• Be gentle when it is wet: wet hair breaks more easily.
• Trim split ends now and then.
• Avoid very tight hairstyles that pull at the roots.
• Eat enough protein and iron; diet affects hair.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Does cutting hair make it grow faster?
A: No, growth happens at the root, not the ends.
Q: Why does my hair stop growing at a certain length?
A: Your growing phase ends and the hair falls out before getting longer.
Q: Is losing hair every day normal?
A: Yes, about 50 to 100 hairs a day is normal.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
The world record for the longest hair is over 5 meters (more than 18 feet)! Xie Qiuping from China grew it for decades. 😳
