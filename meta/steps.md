Title: What If You Walked 10,000 Steps Every Day? 👟 #shorts

Description:
10,000 steps every day. Is it really the magic number? 👟
A study of ~50,000 adults found more steps = lower risk of dying early, but the benefit leveled off around 6,000-8,000 steps (age 60+) and 8,000-10,000 (younger adults).
Plot twist: "10,000" came from a 1960s Japanese pedometer name, not science! 😳 Every extra step helps.
