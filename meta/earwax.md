Ear wax. Kind of gross, right? 👂 What if you never cleaned your ears?
Your ears clean themselves: every time you chew or talk, wax slowly moves out. Cotton swabs can push it deeper.
Plot twist: wet or dry ear wax depends on one tiny gene. Most people in East Asia have the dry kind! 🧬
