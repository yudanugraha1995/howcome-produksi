What if you ate a spoonful of honey every day? 🍯

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
People have used it as food and medicine for thousands of years. Honey has antioxidants and can soothe a sore throat and calm a cough. But a tablespoon still has about sixty calories, and it's mostly sugar.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Honey almost never spoils, and archaeologists found pots of it in ancient Egyptian tombs, thousands of years old, that hadn't gone bad. That's because it's low in water and slightly acidic, so bacteria can't grow. Just never give honey to babies under one year old, because of a rare risk called botulism.

What if you ate a spoonful of honey every day? People have used it as food and medicine for thousands of years. Honey has antioxidants and can soothe a sore throat and calm a cough. But a tablespoon still has about sixty calories, and it's mostly sugar. But here's the twist. Honey almost never spoils, and archaeologists found pots of it in ancient Egyptian tombs, thousands of years old, that hadn't gone bad. That's because it's low in water and slightly acidic, so bacteria can't grow. Just never give honey to babies under one year old, because of a rare risk called botulism. Honey or sugar?
