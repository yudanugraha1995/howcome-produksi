What if you followed the five second rule every day? The rule is so famous that scientists actually decided to test it. Grab it fast and it's fine, right? Lots of people believe a quick grab keeps the germs away. But here's the twist. Scientists tested it, and bacteria can jump onto food in less than one second, especially wet food like watermelon! Carpet passed on fewer germs than tile or steel, and the longer food sits, the more bacteria it picks up. So for wet food, it's more like a zero second rule! Do you follow the five second rule?
