What if you picked your nose every single day? Studies suggest about nine in ten adults do it. Almost everyone does it, and boogers are just mucus that trapped dust and germs. Your nose makes mucus all day to catch what you breathe in. But here's the twist. Picking can scratch the inside of your nose, cause nosebleeds and spread germs, and a study of health workers found nose pickers caught Covid more often.
