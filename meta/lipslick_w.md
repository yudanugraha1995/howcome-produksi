What if you licked your lips all day? 👄

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Cold, dry winter air makes chapped lips even more common. It feels like it helps when they're dry. Lips have no oil glands, so they dry out faster than the rest of your skin.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Saliva evaporates fast and takes moisture with it, so your lips end up even drier and can crack. Doctors call it lip licker's dermatitis. A plain lip balm or petroleum jelly works much better. Kids often get a red ring around their mouth from licking, and doctors see it every winter.
