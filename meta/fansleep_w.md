# What If You Slept With a Fan On Every Night? 🌀

## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Slept With a Fan On Every Night? 🌀

Description:
What if you slept with a fan on every night? 🌀

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Fans are one of the most popular sleep hacks on hot nights. The breeze and white noise help a lot of people sleep. Cool air helps your body drop its temperature, which makes falling asleep easier.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Moving air can dry out your nose, eyes and throat, and blow dust around, so allergies can get worse. And fan death? That's just a myth! Clean the fan blades and point it away from your face. If you have allergies, a fan with a filter or an air purifier can help.
