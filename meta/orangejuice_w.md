# What If You Drank Orange Juice Every Morning? 🍊

## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Drank Orange Juice Every Morning? 🍊

Description:
What if you drank orange juice every single morning? 🍊

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
It's the classic breakfast drink in many homes. One glass gives you more than a full day of vitamin C. It also has potassium and folate.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Orange juice has nearly as much sugar as the same amount of soda, and without the fiber of a whole orange it hits your blood sugar faster. It takes about three oranges to make one glass, so it's easy to drink a lot of sugar fast. Experts suggest eating the whole fruit, or keeping juice to one small glass a day.
