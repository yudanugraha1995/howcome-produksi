Title: What If You Slept With Makeup On Every Night? 💄

Description:
What if you slept with your makeup on every night? 💄

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Foundation, concealer and mascara sit on your skin all day long. It feels harmless after a long day. Many people skip washing when they're tired.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Makeup can clog your pores overnight and lead to breakouts, and mascara left on can irritate your eyes. Your skin repairs itself while you sleep! Even a quick wipe or a gentle cleanser before bed makes a big difference. Makeup on your pillowcase can also spread oil and bacteria back onto your face night after night.
