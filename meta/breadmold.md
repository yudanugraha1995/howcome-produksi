Title: What If You Ate Moldy Bread? 🍞 #shorts

Description:
A green spot on your bread! 🍞 Just cut it off and eat the rest?
Bad idea: mold has thin roots that spread deep into soft bread, far past the spot you see. Moldy soft foods go in the trash.
Plot twist: some mold is great! Blue cheese gets its flavor from mold, and penicillin came from mold too.

Cut it off or toss it? 👇

Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service
