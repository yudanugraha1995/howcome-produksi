Title: What If You Snored Every Night? 😴 #shorts

Zzz... SNORE! 😴 What if you snored every single night? Is it just noisy, or could it be a warning sign? KOMO listens closely.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
When you sleep, the muscles in your throat relax. The airway gets narrower, and air squeezing through makes soft tissues like the soft palate and uvula vibrate. That vibration is the snoring sound. Sleeping on your back, a blocked nose, alcohol, extra weight and tiredness can all make snoring louder.

📌 KEY FACTS
• Snoring is very common, especially in adults and as people get older.
• Loud snoring with gasps, choking or pauses in breathing can be a sign of obstructive sleep apnea.
• In sleep apnea, breathing stops and starts many times a night, which lowers oxygen and breaks up sleep.
• Untreated sleep apnea is linked to daytime sleepiness, high blood pressure and heart problems.
• Sleeping on your side often reduces snoring.
• Alcohol before bed relaxes the throat muscles even more, making snoring worse.

✅ QUIETER NIGHTS
• Try sleeping on your side.
• Avoid alcohol and heavy meals close to bedtime.
• Treat a blocked nose (allergies, colds).
• Keep a regular sleep schedule.
• Loud snoring plus gasping or daytime sleepiness? Talk to a doctor about a sleep study.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Is snoring dangerous?
A: Simple snoring is usually harmless, but snoring with breathing pauses can mean sleep apnea.
Q: Do kids snore?
A: Some do, and frequent loud snoring in kids should be checked by a doctor.
Q: Do anti-snoring gadgets work?
A: Some help some people; a doctor can suggest what fits your cause.
