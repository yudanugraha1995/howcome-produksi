🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you walked into a typical American home? Visitors from abroad notice these things right away. One, carpet in the bedrooms, and sometimes even in the bathroom. Two, a garbage disposal in the kitchen sink that grinds up food scraps. Three, a clothes dryer, because hanging laundry outside is rare, and some neighborhoods even ban it. But here's the twist. Four, light switches flip up for on, while in the UK and Australia the switch usually goes down for on. Five, many homes have no electric kettle, because lower voltage outlets make kettles boil much slower. Instead, lots of people just heat water in the microwave!
