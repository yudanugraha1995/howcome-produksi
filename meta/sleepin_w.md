What if you slept in every weekend? 🛌

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
After a busy week, a few extra hours in bed sounds perfect. It feels like catching up on sleep. On workdays, many people wake up much earlier with an alarm.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Shifting your sleep by a few hours gives you social jet lag, so Monday morning can feel like you just flew across time zones! Sleep experts say keep your wake up time within about an hour, even on weekends. Studies link big weekend sleep shifts to a worse mood and even higher heart risk.
