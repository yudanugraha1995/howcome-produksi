1. What If You Held In Every Fart? 💨 — some gas gets absorbed and leaves through your BREATH
2. What If You Swallowed Spiders in Your Sleep? 🕷️ — the "8 spiders a year" myth; spiders avoid snoring mouths
3. What If You Ate Carrots Until You Turned ORANGE? 🥕 — carotenemia is real (harmless, skin turns orange)
4. What If You Licked a Frozen Pole? 👅 — saliva freezes instantly, tongue gets stuck (use warm water, never pull)
5. What If You Laughed for 10 Minutes Every Day? 😂 — laughter lowers stress hormones, works abs a little
6. What If You Slept With Your Eyes Open? 😳 — nocturnal lagophthalmos, about 1 in 5 people
7. What If You Stared at Your Reflection for 10 Minutes? 🪞 — the "strange-face illusion": your face starts to morph
8. What If You Said Your Name 100 Times? 🗣️ — semantic satiation: words start to sound meaningless
9. What If You Tried to Hum While Holding Your Nose? 👃 — you can't! humming needs air out through the nose
10. What If You Never Burped? 🫧 — "no-burp syndrome" (R-CPD) is real, gurgling chest & bloating
11. What If You Slept Upside Down Like a Bat? 🦇 — blood rushes to your head, eyes & face swell
12. What If You Ate Only Bananas for a Week? 🍌 — bananas are slightly radioactive (potassium-40), but you'd need millions
13. What If You Spun in Circles Every Day? 🌀 — inner-ear fluid keeps spinning; dancers train to "spot"
14. What If You Tried to Lick Your Elbow? 👅 — almost nobody can; tongue + arm too short
15. What If You Drank Pickle Juice Every Day? 🥒 — salty, may ease muscle cramps fast, too much sodium though
