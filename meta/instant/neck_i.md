## Title
What If You Cracked Your Neck Every Day? 😬

## Description
Crack! 😬 What if you cracked your neck every single day? Is that satisfying pop safe, or slowly hurting you? KOMO checks what doctors say.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
Your neck joints are surrounded by fluid. When you twist, the joint space stretches, pressure drops, and gas dissolved in that fluid forms a bubble. That quick change makes the "pop" you hear. It takes about 20 minutes before the same joint can pop again.

📌 KEY FACTS
• The pop comes from gas in the joint fluid, not bones grinding.
• Gentle, occasional neck cracking is usually harmless for most people.
• Forceful, repeated twisting can strain muscles and ligaments.
• In rare cases, sharp, forceful neck moves have been linked to a tear in a neck artery (cervical artery dissection), which can cause a stroke.
• The urge to crack often comes from stiff muscles, poor posture or long phone and desk time.

✅ WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Stretch slowly instead of jerking your head.
• Take breaks from screens and fix your posture.
• See a doctor if cracking comes with pain, swelling, dizziness, numbness or headaches.
