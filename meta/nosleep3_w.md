Title: What If You Didn't Sleep for 3 Days? 😵

Description:
What if you didn't sleep for three days? 😵

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Most adults need about seven to nine hours of sleep every night. After one night, you'd feel slow and moody. Your reaction time slows down like you've been drinking.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
After a few days, people can start seeing things that aren't there. The record is about eleven days, set by a teenager in nineteen sixty four! Doctors say never try this, because sleep is when your brain cleans itself. Even one night without sleep can affect you about as much as being over the legal alcohol limit.

What if you didn't sleep for three days? Most adults need about seven to nine hours of sleep every night. After one night, you'd feel slow and moody. Your reaction time slows down like you've been drinking. But here's the twist. After a few days, people can start seeing things that aren't there. The record is about eleven days, set by a teenager in nineteen sixty four! Doctors say never try this, because sleep is when your brain cleans itself.
