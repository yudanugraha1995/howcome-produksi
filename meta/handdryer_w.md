# What If You Only Used Hand Dryers? 💨

## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Only Used Hand Dryers? 💨

Description:
What if you dried your hands with an air dryer every time? 💨

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Lots of public bathrooms have replaced paper towels with them. It feels cleaner, because you don't touch anything. They also cut paper waste, which is good for the planet.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Studies found that jet air dryers can blow germs from wet hands more than a meter away, spreading them around the bathroom far more than paper towels. Rubbing with paper towels also wipes off germs that washing missed. And if you leave with damp hands, germs spread more easily, so dry them fully.
