# What If You Always Slept on Your Left Side? 🛏️

## SHORT METADATA (current)
Title: What If You Always Slept on Your Left Side? 🛏️

Description:
What if you slept on your left side every night? 🛏️

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
Most adults already sleep on their side. Because of the way your stomach sits, lying on your left can help keep acid from flowing back up. That's why some studies found fewer heartburn symptoms on the left side.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Doctors often recommend the left side during pregnancy, because it can improve blood flow to the baby. Side sleeping can also reduce snoring compared with sleeping on your back. But the best position is still the one that lets you sleep well without pain.
