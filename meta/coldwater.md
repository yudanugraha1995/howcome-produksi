Title: What If You Drank Ice-Cold Water Every Day? 🧊 #shorts

Description:
Ice cubes clinking... but grandma says cold water is bad for you! 🧊 Who's right?
For most healthy people, there's no proof it slows digestion or makes you sick. Your stomach warms it up in minutes.
But in people with migraines, ice water can trigger a headache, so sip slowly.
Plot twist: on a hot day, people cycling in the heat lasted longer with cold drinks! 🚴
