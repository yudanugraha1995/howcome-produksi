Title: What If You Drank Ice-Cold Water Every Day? 🧊 #shorts

Description:
Ice cubes clinking in your glass... but grandma says cold water is bad for you! 🧊 What if you drank ice-cold water every day? Who is right? KOMO investigates.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
When cold water reaches your stomach, your body warms it up to body temperature within minutes. For most healthy people there is no good evidence that cold water slows digestion, "freezes" fat or makes you sick. But cold drinks can affect some people differently, especially people who get migraines or certain throat and esophagus problems.

📌 KEY FACTS
• For most healthy people, there is no proof that cold water harms digestion.
• Your body quickly warms cold water to body temperature.
• In a study of women, ice water held in the mouth triggered a headache more often in those with migraines.
• Some people with a swallowing condition called achalasia feel worse with cold drinks.
• On hot days, cold drinks can help lower body temperature during exercise.
• The best water is the one you actually drink enough of.

✅ HYDRATION TIPS
• Drink the temperature you enjoy; it helps you drink enough.
• If cold water gives you headaches, sip slowly or choose cool instead of icy.
• On hot days, cold water can help you cool down.
• Feel pain when swallowing cold drinks? Ask a doctor.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Does cold water slow your metabolism?
A: No. Warming the water uses a tiny bit of energy, but the effect is very small.
Q: Is warm water better in the morning?
A: If you like it, sure, but there is no strong proof it is healthier.
Q: Can cold water give you a sore throat?
A: Colds come from viruses, not from cold water.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
On a hot day, cold drinks can be a secret weapon: in one study, people cycling in the heat lasted longer when they drank cold drinks instead of warm ones! 🚴
