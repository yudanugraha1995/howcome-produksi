Title: What If You Ate Super Fast Every Day? 🍜 #shorts

Description:
Plate empty in 3 minutes! 🍜 What if you ate super fast every day?
Your stomach needs about 20 minutes to tell your brain you're full. Eat too fast and you keep eating before the signal arrives. Studies link fast eating to weight gain.
Plot twist: putting your fork down between bites helps you feel full sooner!

Source: Ohkuma et al., International Journal of Obesity (2015)
