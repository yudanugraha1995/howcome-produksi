Title: What If You Ate Spicy Food Every Day? 🌶️ #shorts

Description:
Your mouth is on fire, your nose is running, you're sweating. 🌶️ Is it hurting you or helping you?
A study of ~500,000 adults in China found people who ate spicy food almost every day had ~14% lower risk of dying during the study. A clue, not proof!
Plot twist: water won't save you. Capsaicin is oily, so milk works better. 🥛

Sources: Lv et al., BMJ (2015)
