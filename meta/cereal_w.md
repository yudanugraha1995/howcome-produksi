What if you ate cereal for breakfast every morning? 🥣

📌 THE QUICK ANSWER
It's quick, cheap and kids love it. Many cereals are fortified with iron, B vitamins and folic acid, and with milk you get calcium too. Whole grain cereals also add fiber, which keeps you full longer.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Some kids' cereals are more than half sugar by weight, and most people pour way more than one serving. A big cereal bowl can easily hold two or three servings without you noticing. Experts suggest picking one with more fiber and less than about six grams of sugar per serving.

🎙️ FULL SCRIPT
What if you ate cereal for breakfast every morning? It's quick, cheap and kids love it. Many cereals are fortified with iron, B vitamins and folic acid, and with milk you get calcium too. Whole grain cereals also add fiber, which keeps you full longer. But here's the twist. Some kids' cereals are more than half sugar by weight, and most people pour way more than one serving. A big cereal bowl can easily hold two or three servings without you noticing. Experts suggest picking one with more fiber and less than about six grams of sugar per serving.
