Title: What If You Talked in Your Sleep? 😴 #shorts

Description:
Mumble, mumble... 😴 What if you talked in your sleep every night? What are sleep talkers really saying? KOMO opens the recordings.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
Sleep talking (somniloquy) is a kind of parasomnia, which means something unusual happens while you sleep. It can happen in any stage of sleep. Usually the brain is partly asleep and partly active, so speech comes out without you knowing. Stress, lack of sleep, fever or alcohol can make it more likely, and it often runs in families.

📌 KEY FACTS
• Around two thirds of people talk in their sleep at some point in their life.
• It is most common in children and usually harmless.
• Most sleep talkers do not remember it in the morning.
• Sleep deprivation and stress can make it happen more often.
• In a French study, researchers recorded hundreds of sleep-talking episodes from adults.
• Talking with shouting, hitting or acting out dreams can be a sign of another sleep disorder and should be checked.

✅ IF YOU TALK IN YOUR SLEEP
• Keep a regular sleep schedule.
• Get enough sleep: tiredness makes it worse.
• Reduce stress before bed.
• Record yourself with a sleep app if you are curious!

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Can sleep talkers answer questions?
A: Sometimes they mumble a reply, but they usually do not remember.
Q: Do sleep talkers tell the truth?
A: Not necessarily; speech can be random or dream-related.
Q: Should I wake a sleep talker?
A: There is no need; it is usually harmless.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
When scientists recorded sleep talkers, the most common word was "no", and people swore way more in their sleep than when awake! 🤭
