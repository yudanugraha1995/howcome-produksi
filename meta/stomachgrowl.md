Title: What If Your Stomach Growled All Day? 😳 #shorts

Description:
Grrrr! Right in the middle of a quiet class. 😳 What if your stomach growled all day long? KOMO explains where that noise really comes from.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
The growling sound has a name: borborygmi. Your stomach and intestines are muscular tubes that squeeze to move food, liquid and gas along. When they are mostly empty, a cleaning wave called the migrating motor complex sweeps through roughly every 1.5 to 2 hours, pushing leftovers forward. With little food inside to muffle it, the air and liquid make loud rumbles.

📌 KEY FACTS
• The medical name for tummy rumbles is borborygmi.
• The sounds come from muscles squeezing gas and liquid through your gut.
• When you have not eaten for a while, a "housekeeping" wave sweeps through about every 90 to 120 minutes.
• The hunger hormone ghrelin rises before meals and is linked to these waves.
• Gas-producing foods and fizzy drinks can make rumbling louder.
• Loud rumbling with pain, diarrhea or bloating that does not go away is worth checking with a doctor.

✅ QUIET TUMMY TIPS
• Eat regular meals and small snacks.
• Drink water: it can calm rumbles for a bit.
• Eat slowly to swallow less air.
• Go easy on fizzy drinks before quiet moments.

❓ QUICK FAQ
Q: Does a growling stomach always mean hunger?
A: No, it also happens while digesting, just more quietly.
Q: Is a growling stomach bad?
A: Usually not; it is a normal sign your gut is working.
Q: Why is it loudest in the morning?
A: Your gut is empty, so the cleaning waves echo more.

🤯 PLOT TWIST
Your stomach growls even when you are full! You just cannot hear it as well, because food inside muffles the sound. 🍔
