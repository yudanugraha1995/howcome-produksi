Zzz... SNORE! What if you snored every night? Just noisy, or a warning sign?

Snoring is soft tissue in your throat shaking as air squeezes past. Loud snoring with gasps or pauses can mean sleep apnea, when breathing keeps stopping.

Plot twist! Some snores beat ninety decibels, as loud as a lawn mower!
