It's two in the afternoon, and your eyes are closing. What if you took a nap every single afternoon? Lazy, or genius? Let's find out what science says.

That afternoon dip is real. Your body clock naturally lowers your alertness in the early afternoon.

But nap too long and you slide into deep sleep. Wake up from that, and you feel worse, groggy and slow, for a while.

Short naps are the secret. NASA tested it on airline pilots. A nap of about twenty six minutes boosted their performance by thirty four percent, and their alertness by fifty four percent. Huge, for a tiny nap! The trick is to stop before deep sleep, so set an alarm for about twenty minutes.

Plot twist! Try a coffee nap. Drink a coffee, then nap right away for about fifteen minutes. Caffeine takes a while to kick in, so you wake up just as it starts working. Double boost! Just don't nap too late in the day, or you'll be wide awake at bedtime.
